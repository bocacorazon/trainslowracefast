## What to look for

For each provider, check whether the published terms have changed since the last review on:

1. Re-display of athlete-own data
2. Email or web delivery of derived insights
3. AI / ML use of activity content
4. Charging for derivative features
5. Storage retention / deletion requirements
6. Required attribution / branding
7. Rate limits and tier definitions
8. Cross-athlete data handling
9. Partnership tier eligibility (especially Garmin)
