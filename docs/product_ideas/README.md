# Product ideas

Versioned capture of product ideas that emerged during validation and execution work.

These notes are intentionally lightweight. They are not locked roadmap commitments or
technical specs. Their job is to preserve promising product directions so they can be
revisited, compared, refined, or killed later.

## Current ideas

- `01_goal_feasibility_engine.md`: forward-looking engine that estimates what a target
  race time or performance goal usually requires, then compares that to the athlete's
  current training reality.
- `02_within_session_adherence_score.md`: session-level score that rewards executing the
  intended intensity plan rather than merely accumulating load or acute stimulus.
- `03_hr_spike_anomaly_correction.md`: data-quality layer that detects and flags or
  repairs bogus heart-rate spikes before they pollute downstream metrics.
